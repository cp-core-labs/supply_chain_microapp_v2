## Supply Chain Intelligence v2.0.0

This MVP provides a decision-support workspace for exploring demand forecasting, inventory planning, and supply-chain risk.

### Highlights
- Persistent local data storage with SQLite
- On-demand demand forecasting
- Inventory planning and replenishment indicators
- Operational risk alerts and data-confidence signals
- Recommendations and scenario analysis
- CSV exports and refreshed Streamlit interface

### Scope
This is an MVP for analytics and decision support. It is not a production-ready multi-tenant platform or an ERP replacement. Forecasts and recommendations should be reviewed before operational use.
